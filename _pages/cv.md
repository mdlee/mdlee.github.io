## Current position

*Professor of Cognitive Sciences*, University of California, Irvine.

Areas of specialization: computational models of cognition, Bayesian statistics, cognitive data science.

## Education

* 1988–1990. Bachelor of Science, Faculty of Mathematical Sciences, University of Adelaide
* 1992. Bachelor of Arts (Honours, First Class), Department of Psychology, University of Adelaide
* 1993. Graduate Diploma in Education, University of Adelaide
* 1994–1997. Doctor of Philosophy in Cognitive Science, University of Adelaide. Thesis: *The connectionist learning of mental representation.* Advisors: Douglas Vickers (Psychology), Robert Bogner (Electrical and Electronic Engineering). Admitted to the degree 23 April 1998
